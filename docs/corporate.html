Corporate packages

Host team outings, client sessions, or private play at Urban Pickle covered and open courts in Taman U Thant, with food, drinks, and parking nearby.

What we offer

  • Multi-court bookings for groups
  • Covered, open, and ball-machine court rates
  • Paddle rental and balls for sale over the counter
  • On-site amenities: changing rooms, showers, F&B, parking

Venue policy

Booking refunds & changes

  • Strictly no refund or carry forward of the non-utilized sessions.
  • 72 hours advance notice must be given for any change of booking time. Any request to change the booking time is subject to court availability and can only be made once.

Rain-affected booking policy

  • Rain-affected rescheduling requests must be made within 48 hours of the original booking. WhatsApp +60126002178 to reschedule.
  • Rain-affected sessions under 35 mins can be rescheduled to a time within one month from the original booking date, subject to availability.

Others

  • Please do not leave your valuables unattended. We will not be responsible for any theft.
  • Kids 12 y/o and below are allowed to enter strictly with adult supervision only. Please note that the supervising adult is responsible for their safety and behavior.
  • Courtsite/Urban Pickle takes no responsibility for any incidents especially kids aged 12 and below. Parents or guardians should ensure adequate supervision and take responsibility for their child's safety. Any damages caused will be charged accordingly.
